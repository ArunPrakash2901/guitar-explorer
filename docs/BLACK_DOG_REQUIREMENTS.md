## Review on guitar

1. Open the studio reference and step through the arrangement in order. Treat time links as cues and listen on either side of each boundary.
2. In Verse 1, audition both approaches to A, find two playable E4 routes, and count the pickup and displaced accent. Try a different target root without looking at the fretboard labels first.
3. Compare the shorter verses with the full verse, both bridges, and the returning chorus. Predict the transition before hearing it. In Chorus 2, follow the roots with one note of your own, then compare the single and harmonized versions in Verse 3.
4. In the outro, choose a low and high region, identify one contour or articulation that carries between them, and create a short response. Compare the ending in the solo lesson with the studio fade.
5. Return to the song map, resume a section, and confirm your reflection persists. Record one discovery and one transfer you physically attempted. Note any claim or position that needs source review.

Physical acceptance requires the owner to try the reviewed head. Software checks cover the model, navigation and controls. Recording synchronization, source interpretation and playability require the corresponding listening or guitar evidence.
