    Constraint

    Every chord must contain all structural chord tones. Among valid chords, the task asks for the smallest total same-string fret movement from the voicing you actually played.

    Two judgments

    Chord correctness and voice-leading efficiency stay separate. A distant C major remains a real C major.

    Your guitar supplies sound

    The app supplies theory, bounded choices and explanations. It does not generate audio or listen through a microphone.

    Session evidence only

    Attempts, assistance and elapsed seconds stay in memory until restart or page close. They are not a mastery score.

    No generic voicing engine, progression interpreter, persistence, audio, CAGED system or Learning Engine is introduced by this experiment.
